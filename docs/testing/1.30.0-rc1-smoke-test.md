# 1.30.0-rc1: quick device test

Use a test account and test products. Record PASS, FAIL, or NOT TESTED for each step, with the device model, Android version, barcode, network state, and what happened. Do not use participant data or place credentials in screenshots. These device checks have not yet been run on this candidate.

1. **Update without deleting data.** Record two saved Pantry items. Install `outputs/releases/1.30.0-rc1/WhatsOnMyFood-1.30.0-rc1.apk` over the existing locally signed app without uninstalling it. If Android reports a signature mismatch, stop; do not erase study data. Confirm version `1.30.0-rc1` (code `18`), then check the same Pantry items after reopening the app. This also checks the database migration on a real device.
2. **Scan and check the data source.** Scan a product already known to work through Open Food Facts and confirm it still loads. For a USDA fallback check, first confirm the hosted backend reports `usdaKeyConfigured=true`; the September 24 check reported `false`. Then use a product with an exact US branded-food USDA record but no usable Open Food Facts result. Compare its name, brand, barcode, ingredients, and nutrition with the package, and confirm the USDA source label appears. A live database may not contain a particular item; record NOT TESTED rather than guessing. See the [detailed USDA guide](usda-fallback-test-guide.md) for controlled cases.
3. **Check incomplete nutrition.** Open a product missing total sugar, saturated fat, or sodium/salt data. The app should not invent zero values or call the product healthy overall. It should show that an overall score is unavailable while retaining supported findings. Switch to Spanish and French and confirm the message is understandable in each language.
4. **Check AI and recalls.** With AI enabled, scan a product with complete ingredients and review the explanation and source links. Confirm unlimited study access remains available. Open its recall status and retry; an unavailable FDA source should show a clear error, not a safe/no-match claim. On a tablet, confirm the Pantry recall-notification action is visible. See the [previous candidate's broader regression guide](1.28-rc1-smoke-test.md) for notifications, badges, alternatives, and navigation.
5. **Check failures and recovery.** Turn off the network, retry lookup/recall actions, and confirm clear errors and usable navigation. Reconnect and retry. Do not call a cached answer a successful new request.

Do not distribute as a fully accepted USDA-enabled candidate until the hosted key is configured and steps 1–4 pass on a device. No Play Console acceptance or upload is implied by this checklist.
