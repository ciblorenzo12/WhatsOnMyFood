This is the first Beta 1.0 build prepared for the Milestone 6 advisor demonstration and participant testing. It is a signed Android release candidate, not a public production release.

## Demonstrated workflows

- Barcode scanning and product lookup
- Ingredient-label capture and OCR
- Deterministic health result and GOOD, WATCH, and INFO findings
- Bitwise AI plain-language explanation with source-aware fallback behavior
- Add to Pantry, remove, sorting, and product reopen
- Product refresh and honest online, cache, stale, and offline states
- Marketplace comparison and controlled unavailable states

## Beta-specific behavior

The normal three-per-day Bitwise freemium restriction is disabled in this Beta so test participants can complete every assigned AI-supported scan. Billing startup and usage counting remain bypassed while `UNLIMITED_AI_TESTING=true`. The underlying freemium policy remains covered by tests and can be restored after participant testing.

The app now warms the hosted Bitwise service at startup and permits two bounded automatic cold-start retries. If the service still cannot respond, deterministic product findings remain visible and the Bitwise card offers a manual retry.

## Known beta limitations

- Live product, Gemini, map, and retailer results require network access and valid hosted-service configuration.
- Product-source records can change after this build; run the documented barcode preflight before the advisor session.
- Marketplace development samples must be identified as samples, not current store inventory.
- This beta was rehearsed on the SM-X800 tablet. Broader phone and API-26 coverage remains a later release activity.
