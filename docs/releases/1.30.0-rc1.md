# What's On My Food 1.30.0-rc1

Local study/test release candidate prepared September 24, 2026. This is a signed build for controlled testing, not a public release. It has not been uploaded, published, tagged, or installed on a device in this preparation. Unlimited Bitwise AI access remains enabled, as in the previous study candidate.

## What changed

- The app can ask the protected backend for an exact USDA FoodData Central branded-food match when Open Food Facts cannot provide a usable product. The USDA key remains on the server. A missing nutrient stays unknown rather than being treated as zero, and the app shows the source of returned data.
- An overall nutrition rating is withheld when comparable total sugar, saturated fat, or sodium/salt values are missing. Available findings can still be reviewed. The missing-information message is available in English, Spanish, and French.
- The Bitwise icon stops animating when AI is idle, reducing unnecessary screen work. The tablet Pantry layout again shows the recall-notification action.
- Existing recall, badge, and AI workflows remain in this candidate. These changes do not establish food or allergy safety; compare important information with the package and official source.

## Acceptance still required

On September 24, the configured live backend returned a healthy/readiness response and advertised the USDA route, but reported `usdaKeyConfigured=false`. The app's USDA fallback therefore **has not passed live acceptance**. Configure `FDC_API_KEY` on that hosted backend, restart it, confirm the readiness flag, then test an exact branded-food lookup. Do not put the key in Android or a client request. No backend deployment was performed for this release preparation.

No Android device was connected. Upgrade/data-preservation testing, the Room 12-to-13 migration, real barcode scanning, fallback/source display, recall controls, and AI behavior still need device checks. A successful build and automated tests do not establish those outcomes. This candidate was not pushed or published.
