# What's On My Food 1.19-rc-2

This candidate includes the RC1 UI and profile changes and fixes contradictory product scores and verdicts.

## Changes

- Both product detail screens derive their overall verdict from the deterministic rule report. AI does not independently replace that verdict.
- Blocking rule findings cap the numeric score at 69, below the app's 70+ Good band, even when positive adjustments offset penalties. This is an application scoring policy, not a medical threshold.
- Scores below 70 cannot display a Healthy overall verdict. Missing analysis or ingredients displays Needs Review and an unavailable ring.
- The ring uses score-dependent colors, and the calculation explains the raw score and any blocking-findings cap.
- Three regression tests cover offsetting positive findings, low-score verdict consistency, and missing/clean analysis.
